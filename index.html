INSYNC — Dance Club of IIT Bombay

INSYNC is the official dance club under the Institute Cultural Council (ICC), IIT Bombay. The club promotes creativity, expression, and teamwork through performances, competitions, workshops, and cultural events across campus.

Major Events

AIDS — Annual INSYNC Dance Show

INSYNC's flagship dance production featuring stunning choreography, storytelling, lighting, and large-scale performances.

Battlegrid

A high-energy street dance competition featuring talented performers and crews.

Mood Indigo Performances

Performances during IIT Bombay's iconic cultural festival, Mood Indigo.
